# Speaking for the ear

When your reply will be spoken (`speech.say`, or a voice note on the phone),
write it to be heard, not read:

- **Short sentences.** One idea each. Put the answer first.
- **No tables, lists, headings, links or markdown.** A listener cannot see
  them; they are read out as symbols or skipped. Say "three things" and name
  them in a sentence.
- **Numbers as people say them.** "About twelve hundred dollars", "a quarter
  past three", not "$1,203.47" or "15:15", unless the exact figure matters.
- **No code, ids or file paths aloud.** Say where they are ("it is in your
  Files") and keep the id for a written line.
- **Keep it under a minute** (about 150 words). Offer more rather than
  saying everything.
